2025 – 2026 Python MCWF Quantum optics

Grid-based MCWF solver for open quantum systems

Open-source · soft-mcwf

A Split-Operator Fourier Transform Monte Carlo Wave Function solver that works directly on a momentum-space grid, avoiding Fock-basis truncation issues for anharmonic potentials. Applied to Yb-171 atoms in optical lattices and Gaussian tweezers under continuous 399 nm imaging: the density matrix transitions from coherent to diagonal as the atom heats and delocalises, marking the quantum-to-classical crossover.

2025 – 2026 Python TDVP Variational methods

Sum-of-Gaussians TDVP for multimode open systems

Open-source · multimode-tdvp

Approximates quantum dynamics by restricting to a variational sum-of-Gaussians (SoG) manifold evolved under the McLachlan TDVP principle. The ansatz is non-Gaussian overall, scales as O(NGauss × d) instead of exponentially with mode number, and improves with more Gaussian components. Lindblad dissipation is handled via quantum trajectories with jump operators applied analytically within the manifold. Benchmarked against QuTiP on Kerr oscillators and sideband cooling.

Based on Hackl et al., SciPost Phys. 9, 048 (2020) and Bond et al., J. Chem. Phys. 161, 184113 (2024).

2025 – 2026 Python Semiclassical Laser cooling

Zeeman slower + 2D MOT design for Yb-171

Open-source · azs-2dmot

Semiclassical trajectory simulation of an atomic Zeeman slower and 2D magneto-optical trap for Yb-171 on the broad 399 nm transition. Two scattering-rate models (shared saturation and seff) bracket the true multi-beam rate. Parameters - beam detuning, power, and magnet geometry - are optimised via two-stage Bayesian optimisation (scikit-optimize), maximising the capture velocity into the MOT.
